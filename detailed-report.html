Manu Yantralaya Image Provenance

Comparison of product photographs on supremalprecision.com against manujaipur.com. Evidence collected 19 September 2026, IST. Prepared for Manu Yantralaya, Jaipur.

Five of the seven product photographs on the Supremal Precision products page are exact copies of photographs from the Manu Yantralaya website. Every one of the five is pixel-for-pixel identical to the Manu file, with the same dimensions and the same internal JPEG compression tables. Two of the five are byte-for-byte identical to files that the Internet Archive captured on manujaipur.com on 6 May 2019, more than six years before the supremalprecision.com domain existed.

Four of the accompanying product descriptions on the Supremal page also reproduce Manu's wording nearly verbatim.

Why "pixel-identical" matters

A resized, cropped or re-photographed copy would show small pixel differences. Here the difference is zero on every pixel of every pair, and the JPEG quantization tables inside the files are identical. That only happens when the file itself was copied. For three pairs, Supremal's copy has been re-saved as a progressive JPEG, which changes the bytes but not a single pixel. For the other two, Supremal's file is the same file, down to the last byte, as the one the Internet Archive recorded on Manu's site in 2019.

How this was established

  1. Downloaded every image referenced by the five pages of supremalprecision.com (9 files) and every image in Manu's public WordPress media library (474 files, with the library's own upload timestamps).
  2. Compared each Supremal image against all 474 Manu images using three perceptual hashes (pHash, dHash, wHash at 16-bit), then confirmed each candidate with a full pixel-by-pixel diff and a comparison of the JPEG quantization tables.
  3. Computed MD5 and SHA-256 of every file. Two Supremal files hash identically to Manu files.
  4. Queried the Internet Archive's CDX index for manujaipur.com, downloaded the raw 2019 captures of the two polyamide cage photos, and confirmed they hash identically to Supremal's 2026 files.
  5. Pulled WHOIS creation dates for both domains and the hosting details for supremalprecision.com.

Anyone can repeat the two decisive checks in a terminal:

curl -sL -o supremal_crb.jpg  https://supremalprecision.com/assets/img/product-crb-cage.jpeg
curl -sL -o manu_crb_2019.jpg "http://web.archive.org/web/20190506083756id_/http://www.manujaipur.com/images/polyamide_cage_003.jpg"
md5 supremal_crb.jpg manu_crb_2019.jpg        # both: 34cb4caa5018bc771a90cd2f017caa99

curl -sL -o supremal_crb2.jpg https://supremalprecision.com/assets/img/product-crb-cage-2.jpeg
curl -sL -o manu_crb2_2019.jpg "http://web.archive.org/web/20190506082911id_/http://www.manujaipur.com/images/polyamide_cage_004.jpg"

Limits of this evidence

  • Who owns the copyright. The evidence shows Manu published these photos years before Supremal existed and that Supremal's files are copies of Manu's. Under the Indian Copyright Act, the first owner of a commissioned photograph is normally the person who commissioned it, unless the photographer's contract says otherwise. Manu should confirm who took the photos and on what terms before sending a legal notice.
  • Three photos are proven back to March 2025 only. The ball, hub and taper cage photos are documented on Manu's site from 7 March 2025 by WordPress upload records, still six months before Supremal's domain existed. A search of the older archived site for earlier copies was hampered by Internet Archive rate limits and is noted separately.
  • Supremal's own archive history is unknown. The Internet Archive could not be queried for supremalprecision.com during collection, so the first date Supremal published the photos is unknown. It cannot be earlier than 8 September 2025.
  • Two Supremal images are unaccounted for. The two cylinder-sleeve images did not come from Manu's site.

Recommended next steps

Submit supremalprecision.com/products.php, index.php and the five image URLs to the Internet Archive's Save Page Now (web.archive.org/save) so a neutral third party holds a dated copy. Keep the downloaded files and the hash manifest unchanged. Screenshots with a visible date and URL bar help too.
  2. Confirm ownership. Locate the original camera files or the photographer's invoice. Original RAW or full-resolution files that Supremal cannot produce are the strongest ownership proof.
  3. Send a written notice to Supremal Precision at the address above, listing the five files and demanding removal within a fixed period. Supremal is a 2025 company in the same industrial area of Jaipur, so a direct letter is likely to be effective.
  4. If ignored, notify the host. Data Ingenious Global Limited can be sent a takedown notice with the hash manifest and this report.
  5. Legal route if needed. Copying a photograph without permission is infringement under Section 51 of the Copyright Act, 1957, with civil remedies under Section 55. A lawyer can add the copied text as a second ground.

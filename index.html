Photo evidence · Manu Yantralaya, Jaipur · prepared 19 September 2026

Supremal Precision's website is using Manu Yantralaya's product photos.

Five of the seven product photos on supremalprecision.com are exact copies of photos from manujaipur.com. Manu had them online years before Supremal existed. The pictures below show it.

What next

What Manu can do now

1

Freeze the evidence

Ask the Internet Archive to save Supremal's product page today (web.archive.org/save). Keep the files from this report unchanged.

2

Find the original photos

The camera files or the photographer's invoice prove who owns the pictures.

3

Write to Supremal

A formal letter to their Jaipur address listing the five photos and asking for removal by a fixed date.

4

If ignored, go higher

Notify their web host (Data Ingenious Global Ltd) and, if needed, a lawyer. Copying photos without permission is infringement under India's Copyright Act, 1957.

How this was checked. Every image on both websites was downloaded on 19 September 2026 and compared dot by dot by computer. File fingerprints (MD5 and SHA-256) were computed for each file and for the Internet Archive's 2019 copies. Website registration dates come from public WHOIS records. The original files and a fingerprint list are kept alongside this document.

Two of Supremal's product photos (the two cylinder sleeves) did not come from Manu's website, and their source is unknown. Nothing in this document is legal advice.

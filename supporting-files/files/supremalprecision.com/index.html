About Us

Five decades of precision engineering

Founded by Mr. Narendra Chandra Mathur — whose journey began in 1972 with brass lamp holders and evolved into a trusted vendor network for deep-groove ball bearings — Supremal Precision carries forward over 50 years of manufacturing heritage in precise bearing parts.

Today we supply polyamide bearing cages and precision components to multinational bearing majors including SKF, FAG, Timken, NBC and Nachi, operating from our facility at Vishwakarma Industrial Area, Jaipur.
